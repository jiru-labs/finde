      How it is calculated. Driving times come from the Valhalla route planner on OpenStreetMap data, without traffic. Each leg gets 15 min added to get into town and park, plus 15 min for every 2 h of continuous driving, the break recommended by Spain's traffic authority (DGT). You set the time in each city; the default is what it usually takes to see the essentials on foot. In the city where you sleep, that time is split between Saturday afternoon and Sunday morning.

      Tolls. The page shows the kilometres on toll roads according to OpenStreetMap and how much time it costs to avoid them; the price is not calculated because it depends on the stretch and the time of day. With “Avoid tolls” the whole plan is recalculated on the free alternative.
